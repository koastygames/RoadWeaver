# The Wither Dimension

A Fabric 26.2 late-game expansion centered on the Wither boss.

## Progression
1. Defeat the vanilla Wither and obtain a Nether Star.
2. Craft Withered Obsidian and a Soul Brazier.
3. Build a 5x6 Withered Obsidian frame immediately north of the Soul Brazier.
4. Use a Nether Star on the Soul Brazier to activate the Wither Gate.
5. Enter the Wither Dimension, explore four biomes, defeat custom mobs, raid landmarks and mine Witherite/Soul Vein ore.
6. Refine Witherite into equipment slightly above Netherite, with Soul Cores used for end-game recipes.

## Balance targets
- Dimension entry costs a Nether Star.
- Witherite ore requires diamond-tier or better mining capability.
- Witherite is deliberately one step above Netherite, not a creative-tier material.
- Powerful gear requires both ore progression and mob/structure loot.

Requires Minecraft 26.2, Fabric Loader 0.19.3+ and Fabric API.
